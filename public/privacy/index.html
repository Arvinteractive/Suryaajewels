Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026

Suryaa Jewels Craft ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to showcase our handcrafted 22K gold jewelry and to help visitors find and reach our atelier in Coimbatore. This policy explains what happens to information when you visit suryaajewels.in.

We keep this simple because the website itself is simple: it is a showcase site with no accounts, no online checkout, and no contact form. There is very little for us to collect in the first place.

Information we do not collect

This website does not ask you to register, sign in, or submit any form. We do not collect your name, email address, or payment details through the site, and we do not run any advertising or behavioural tracking on it.

Information stored in your browser

When you use the language switch (English / Hindi / Tamil), your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key suryaa.lang so the site remembers it on your next visit. This value never leaves your device — it is not sent to us or to any third party, and it contains no personal information. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

Embedded third-party content

A few elements on this page are loaded directly from third-party services, which may process technical data (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies, independent of us:

  • Google Fonts — the Outfit and Raleway typefaces (and Noto Sans Devanagari / Tamil, when those languages are selected) are loaded from Google's font servers.
  • Google Maps — the map embedded in the "Visit" section of the homepage is served directly by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Instagram — links to our Instagram profile take you to Instagram's site, which has its own privacy practices.

We don't control what these third parties do with that data — please refer to their own privacy policies for details.

Server logs

Like virtually all websites, our hosting provider automatically records standard technical information for every visit — such as IP address, browser type, and access times — as part of normal web server operation and security monitoring. We do not use this information for tracking or marketing.

Cookies

This website does not set any advertising or analytics cookies. The only client-side storage in use is the local storage entry described above, which is not a cookie and is not used for tracking.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this site.

Your information when you contact us

If you call, visit our atelier, or message us on Instagram, any information you share with us in that conversation (such as your name, phone number, or jewelry preferences) is handled directly by us as part of that relationship, and by the respective platform (e.g. Instagram/Meta) where applicable — not through this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website or applicable law. The "Effective date" above will always reflect the latest revision.
